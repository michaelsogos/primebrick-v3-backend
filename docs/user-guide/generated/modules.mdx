List, search and manage all services.

The page is visible with the `access.system.settings.modules` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/system/settings/modules` — List, search and manage all services. *(this page)*
- `/system/settings/modules/{code}` — View and edit an existing service registry.

## What you can do here

### How to edit a service
1. Open the row actions menu on the service and click **Edit**.
2. Update the fields — required: **active Tab**.
3. Click **Save** — the changes to the service are saved.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | DISABLED | — | Creates a new service | no |
| **update** | ENABLED | — | Opens the service for editing | no |
| **delete** | DISABLED | — | Marks the service as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | DISABLED | — | Brings a soft-deleted service back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the service forever | no |
| **audit history** | DISABLED | — | Shows the change history of the service | no |

- Creating a new service is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on services.
- Editing a service is available on this page — the row action opens the record in a new browser tab.
- Soft-deleting a service is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on services.
- Restoring a soft-deleted service is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on services.
- Permanently deleting a service is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on services.
- Viewing the change history of a service is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on services.

### Bulk actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
