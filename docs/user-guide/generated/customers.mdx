List, search and manage all customers.

Each row shows the Onboarded At.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/customers` — List, search and manage all customers. *(this page)*
- `/crm/customers/create` — Create a new customer.

## What you can do here

### How to delete a customer
1. Open the row actions menu on the customer and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog — the customer is soft-deleted and can be restored.

### How to restore a deleted customer
1. Set the deleted-records filter to **Deleted only** or **All records** to show recoverable records.
2. Open the row actions menu on the customer and click **Restore**.
3. Confirm in the dialog — the customer is active again.

### How to view the change history of a customer
1. Open the row actions menu on the customer and click **Audit history**.
2. The version history of the customer will appear on the right sheet panel.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | DISABLED | `customer.create.single` | Creates a new customer | no |
| **update** | DISABLED | `customer.update.single` | Opens the customer for editing | no |
| **delete** | ENABLED | `customer.delete.single` | Marks the customer as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `customer.restore.single` | Brings a soft-deleted customer back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the customer forever | no |
| **audit history** | ENABLED | `customer.read.audit` | Shows the change history of the customer | no |

- Creating a new customer is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `customer.create.single` permission.
- Editing a customer is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `customer.update.single` permission.
- Soft-deleting a customer is available on this page — marks the customer as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored and it requires the `customer.delete.single` permission.
- Restoring a soft-deleted customer is available on this page — brings a soft-deleted customer back and it requires the `customer.restore.single` permission.
- Permanently deleting a customer is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on customers.
- Viewing the change history of a customer is available on this page — every customer keeps a full version history and it requires the `customer.read.audit` permission.

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |

- Duplicating the selected customers is available on this page — duplicates the selected customers and it requires the `customer.duplicate.bulk` permission.
- Exporting the list is available on this page — exports the list and it requires the `customer.export` permission.
